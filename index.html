End-to-End Talent Fulfillment & Partnerships

How We Execute Client & Vendor Requirements

When a corporate client or prime vendor shares an open requirement with Cognisys, our specialized talent acquisition engine activates immediately. We source, thoroughly evaluate through our mandatory 2-Level Screening Process, and submit only high-precision candidate matches.

01

Requirement Intake

Clients or approved vendors send their technical job mandates, skill matrices, and compliance terms (C2C, W2, 1099, C2H, or Full-Time) directly to our account desk.

02

Precision Sourcing

Our recruitment pods map the mandate against our internal bench, pre-vetted talent network, and active sourcing channels across India and the United States.

03

2-Level Screening

Level 1: HR, communication, background, and mandate fitment check.
Level 2: Deep technical & live-coding assessment by senior domain architects.

04

Client / Vendor Submission

Only candidates who pass both screening levels and match 100% of the requirement are submitted to the client or prime vendor for final interview and rapid onboarding.
