How Our Staffing Engine Works

2-Level Screening & Vendor Empanelment (India & USA)

When a client or approved vendor sends a job requirement to Cognisys, our specialized talent acquisition team sources top-tier candidates, puts them through a mandatory 2-Level Screening Process (Level 1 HR/Compliance & Communication Vetting + Level 2 Senior Architect Technical Assessment), and submits only verified, high-match profiles to the client or vendor. We actively welcome Direct Client Vendorship, Corporate Empanelment, Procurement Registration, and Sub-Vendorship from approved vendors across both India and the USA.
